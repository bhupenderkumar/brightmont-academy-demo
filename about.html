Our Mission & History

Founded in 2004, Brightmont Academy has a legacy of providing high-quality education to students with special needs in Phoenix, AZ. Our commitment to small class sizes and personalized instruction allows us to tailor our approach to each child's unique needs.

At Brightmont Academy, we believe in a holistic approach to education that encompasses social-emotional learning, academic rigor, and character development. Our expert faculty use evidence-based methods to foster a love of learning and a growth mindset.

Beyond academics, Brightmont Academy offers a range of extracurricular activities and character-building programs that promote social skills, teamwork, and leadership development. Our students participate in sports, clubs, and community service projects to become well-rounded individuals.
