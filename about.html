Our Mission & History

Founded in 2008, Brightmont Academy has been serving the Phoenix community with a commitment to providing exceptional Special Education services. Our experienced staff has a deep understanding of the needs of students with diverse learning styles and abilities.

Our teaching approach is centered around a student-centered philosophy, where each child's strengths and challenges are carefully considered to create a personalized learning plan. This approach enables our students to build confidence, develop skills, and achieve academic success.

Beyond academics, we believe in fostering a well-rounded student through extracurricular activities and character development programs. Our students participate in arts, athletics, and community service projects, helping them develop essential life skills and values.
