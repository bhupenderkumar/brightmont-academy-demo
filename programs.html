Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program provides a nurturing environment for children aged 3-5, focusing on social-emotional development, fine motor skills, and early literacy and math skills.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students in grades K-5, emphasizing core subjects like reading, writing, math, science, and social studies, while also promoting social-emotional growth and character development.

📜

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School program serves students in grades 6-8, providing a challenging curriculum that prepares students for high school and beyond. We focus on academic rigor, critical thinking, and character development.

🔬

STEM

Our STEM program integrates science, technology, engineering, and math to provide students with a hands-on, project-based learning experience that fosters creativity, problem-solving, and critical thinking.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program offers students the opportunity to explore their creativity through music, art, drama, and dance. We believe that arts education is essential for developing self-expression, confidence, and cultural awareness.

🏈

Athletics

Our Athletics program provides students with the opportunity to develop physical skills, teamwork, and sportsmanship while competing in local and regional competitions. We emphasize fun, fitness, and fair play.
